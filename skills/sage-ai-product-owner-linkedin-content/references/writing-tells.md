# Writing tells

These are habits that make a post read as machine writing. They come from the patterns in Wikipedia's Signs of AI writing. The skill carries its own list so a run does not depend on another skill being installed. The validator checks the word and phrase tells. Sage_AI reviews the structural tells by eye before it delivers.

Where the Product Owner really says a word in the transcript, it is their word. The content profile can list such a word as allowed, and the validator then skips it.

## Checked by the validator

Fix each one by stating the plain fact.

- Stock AI words: additionally, align with, bolster, crucial, deep dive, delve, enduring, enhance, garner, interplay, intricate, intricacies, landscape, meticulous, pivotal, quietly, robust, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant. Also game changer, cutting edge, world class, best in class, seamless, supercharge, unleash, unlock the, next level, and navigate the.
- Sayings that sound deep: at its core, what really matters, the real question is, the heart of the matter, the deeper issue.
- Staged openers: let's dive in, let's explore, let's break this down, here's what you need to know, here's the thing, the thing is, let's be honest, real talk, honestly with a question mark, without further ado, don't get me wrong, to be clear, I'm not saying, this is not to say, read that again, let that sink in.
- Inflated significance: stands as a testament, a pivotal moment, plays a key role, indelible mark, the future looks bright, exciting times ahead, a step in the right direction.
- Chatbot residue: I hope this helps, great question, as an AI, feel free to, certainly with an exclamation mark, I'd be happy to, in conclusion, in summary.
- The not X but Y contrast: not just X but Y, not only, not merely, it's not X, it's Y, this does not mean X. State the point directly. A contrast is fine when the first half corrects a belief the reader really holds.
- A trailing riding phrase: a comma followed by highlighting, underscoring, emphasizing, ensuring, reflecting, symbolizing, contributing to, cultivating, fostering, encompassing, or showcasing.

## Reviewed by Sage_AI

The validator cannot see these. Read every post for them before delivering.

- A one line closer that repeats the point. Cut it, unless it adds a new fact. Fixed template closers stay.
- A forced triad. Three items that do not hold three ideas. Cut to the real number.
- Repeated sentence openings. Several sentences in a row that start the same way. Merge them or change the subject.
- A row of fragments. Three or four fragments in a row that could be one sentence with a specific claim.
- An argument with no one. A denial of something nobody said. State the claim.
- The same closer after every post. Vary it, or cut it.

## Left out on purpose

Stacked qualifiers, hyphenated pairs, passive voice, and curly quotes are weak signs and are not checked. Do not remove them from a Product Owner's own words.

## The fixed lines

A template's fixed lines stay as written, even where they contain a tell. The template ID tracks performance, and changing the line changes what is measured.
