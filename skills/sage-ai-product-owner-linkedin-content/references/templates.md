# Post templates A to O

Fifteen standard templates. The template IDs are fixed. Under no circumstances is a template ID altered. The ID tracks post performance with metrics collected from LinkedIn, and the library grows and shrinks over time as templates are added and poor performers are removed.

How to read a template:

- Notes in double parentheses are notes to you. They never appear in a post.
- A variable between percent signs is replaced with content. No percent sign survives in a post.
- A line that is not a variable is a fixed line. Keep fixed lines as written. Two exceptions: where a template shows a bullet dot, use a character from the library instead, and where a template shows a symbol variable, choose one library character and use it for the whole list.
- Stick to the format of the template in spirit. Take creative license to make the template work with the content, and expand the post where the story warrants it.
- Fixed lines that cannot be written in under 10 words are exempt from the sentence check, and the validator knows them. CF_LI_Post_D "The future of %Industry/Topic% is arriving faster than we think." CF_LI_Post_E "In my humble opinion, you will get better results this way." CF_LI_Post_F "One of the great things about having experience with %topic% is you learn %key insight%." CF_LI_Post_O "This experience reminded us that even small wins can lead to significant improvements." and "It might seem small, but it's made a big difference!" Every other line must fit under 10 words with its variables filled. Where a variable makes a line too long, shorten the variable or take creative license with the line.
- Image text and share text appear at the end of every template. In the import file they go in the notes field and in the image card. See import-format.md.

The Product Owner's content profile can add templates of their own with their own IDs. A profile template never reuses an ID from A to O.
