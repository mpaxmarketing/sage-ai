# Interview protocol

Use this when the Product Owner has no transcript and asks Sage_AI to interview them. The interview ends as a transcript, and the same process then reads it. Sage_AI leads, as in Interview Mode. The Product Owner can answer by voice or by text.

## Purpose

Draw out stories, experiences, knowledge, and opinions that fit the templates. The templates reward a story with a turning point, a lesson learned the hard way, a before and after, a belief that goes against common wisdom, a prediction, and a small win. A thin answer makes a thin post.

## How to run it

1. Say how it works in two sentences. Sage_AI asks one question at a time. The Product Owner answers at length, and Sage_AI does not reflect the answer back.
2. Ask the first question. Wait for the full answer. At a pause, say got it and ask the next question. Ask a follow up only when an answer holds a story with no ending, a claim with no example, or a number with no context.
3. Cover several groups. Aim for enough material for 15 topics. That usually takes 15 to 25 answers of a minute or more. A single answer can supply more than one topic.
4. Keep every question in the record. A question is a scaffold for a topic, and a new topic starts with a new question.
5. Fill in the bracketed topic in each question from the Product Owner's product and field. Skip a question that does not fit.
6. When the Product Owner says they are done, or the material is enough, save the exchange as a transcript. Say so, and go on to the next step of the workflow.

## Questions

### Stories and experiences

1. Can you tell me about a moment in your career or business journey that changed your perspective on [topic]?
2. What was the biggest challenge you faced while building your product or business, and how did you overcome it?
3. What is a decision you made that felt risky at first but turned out to be the right one?
4. Can you share a story about a mistake you made early on and what it taught you?
5. What is a problem you solved recently that you are proud of? How did you approach it?
6. Have you had a moment where everything seemed to click for your business? What led to that turning point?
7. Can you describe a time when someone gave you advice that had a profound impact?

### Insights and opinions

1. What do you believe is the most overlooked aspect of [topic or industry]?
2. What is a common misconception about [your field, your product, or your customers]?
3. If you could change one thing about the industry, what would it be and why?
4. What is your unique perspective on [current trend or challenge in the industry]?
5. What principles guide your decisions when you are stuck or unsure?
6. What is a lesson you have learned about leadership, and how did you learn it?
7. What do you strongly believe that goes against conventional wisdom in your industry?

### Process and knowledge

1. What is the first step you take when tackling a big project or challenge?
2. Can you walk me through how you developed [a specific product, strategy, or system]?
3. What is a tool, system, or habit that has been a game changer for your productivity or success?
4. What mistake do you see others in your industry making, and how would you suggest they approach things differently?
5. How do you prioritize or decide what to focus on in your business?
6. What does your typical day or week look like, and how do you balance competing priorities?

### Reflection and values

1. What motivates you to keep going when things get tough?
2. How has your perspective on success changed over the course of your career?
3. What value or belief drives everything you do in your business?
4. What does impact mean to you, and how do you measure it in your work?
5. If you could give one piece of advice to someone just starting out, what would it be?
6. What is something you wish you had known earlier in your journey?

### Customers and market

1. What do you wish more people understood about your customers or market?
2. Can you share a story about a time when a customer's feedback or experience helped shape your product or business?
3. What is unique about the way you approach serving your customers or clients?
4. What unmet need do you see in your market, and how are you addressing it?
5. How do you identify and solve problems for your customers?

### Vision and future

1. What is a big goal or dream you have for the future, and how are you working toward it?
2. What trends or changes do you predict will shape your industry in the next five years?
3. What legacy do you hope to leave behind with your work or business?
4. What excites you most about what is next for you or your company?
